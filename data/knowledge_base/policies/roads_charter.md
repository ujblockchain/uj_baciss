# Roads Service Charter

*Sample content for training only. Not real municipal policy.*

## Potholes
Potholes on main roads are repaired within 10 working days, and on residential streets within 30 working days. Potholes near schools or on bus routes are repaired first.

## Traffic signals
Faulty traffic signals are repaired within 24 hours.

## Storm and flood damage
Roads damaged by storms or flooding are assessed within 48 hours.

## Road signs and markings
Damaged signs and faded road markings are fixed within 30 working days.
