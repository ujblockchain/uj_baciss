# Response Time Rules by Urgency

*Sample content for training only. Not real municipal policy.*

## Critical urgency
Acknowledge within 1 hour. A crew is on site within 4 hours. A person reviews the report straight away.

## High urgency
Acknowledge within 4 hours. Action starts within 24 hours.

## Medium urgency
Acknowledge within 2 working days. Resolve within 5 working days.

## Low urgency
Acknowledge within 5 working days. Resolve within 30 days.

## Which time applies
Where a service charter gives a shorter time, the charter applies. A report whose area cannot be matched to a municipality goes to a person for review.
