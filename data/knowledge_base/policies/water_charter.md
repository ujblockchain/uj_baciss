# Water Service Charter

*Sample content for training only. Not real municipal policy.*

## No water supply
Report a total loss of water to the Water and Sanitation department. A crew is sent within 4 hours for critical sites (clinics, hospitals, schools) and within 24 hours for households. If an outage lasts more than 24 hours, the municipality provides water tankers.

## Burst pipes and leaks
Burst mains and large leaks in the street are repaired within 48 hours. Leaks on a private property are the owner's responsibility. Give the street name and the nearest cross street when reporting.

## Low water pressure
Low pressure is investigated within 5 working days. Pressure may drop at peak times (06:00 to 08:00 and 17:00 to 20:00).

## Water quality
Discoloured or foul-smelling water must be reported immediately. Residents should not drink it until it is tested. Test results are shared within 2 working days.
