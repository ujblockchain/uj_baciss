# Response Templates

*Sample content for training only. Not real municipal policy.*

## Acknowledgement
Dear resident, thank you for reporting [issue] in [area]. Your reference is [reference]. The [department] department will respond within [time]. For updates call [phone].

## Known planned outage
Dear resident, the [service] interruption in [area] is planned. It runs from [start] to [end]. No action is needed from you. We apologise for the inconvenience.

## Duplicate report
Dear resident, thank you for your report. We already have a report about [issue] in [area] (reference [reference]). We will update you when it is resolved.

## More information needed
Dear resident, thank you for reporting [issue]. To help us find the problem, please send the street name and the nearest cross street. Call [phone] if it is an emergency.
