# Sanitation Service Charter

*Sample content for training only. Not real municipal policy.*

## Sewage overflows
Overflowing sewage is a health risk. A crew responds within 4 hours. After the blockage is cleared, the area is cleaned and disinfected.

## Blocked drains
Blocked street drains are cleared within 3 working days.

## Public toilets
Public toilets are cleaned daily. Broken or closed public toilets are repaired within 5 working days.

## Informal settlements
Communal toilets in informal settlements are serviced twice a week.
