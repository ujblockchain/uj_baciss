# Waste Service Charter

*Sample content for training only. Not real municipal policy.*

## Refuse collection
Household refuse is collected once a week. If a collection is missed, report it within 2 days. The refuse is then collected within 3 working days.

## Illegal dumping
Illegally dumped waste is removed within 14 days. Give the exact location when reporting.

## Bulk waste
Large items such as old furniture must be booked. A bulk collection is arranged within 10 working days.

## Recycling
Recyclables are collected every second week in areas with a recycling service.
