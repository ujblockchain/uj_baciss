# Electricity Service Charter

*Sample content for training only. Not real municipal policy.*

## Power outages
Faults are attended to within 6 hours. Load-shedding follows a published schedule, so report a fault only if the power is off outside the schedule. Customers who depend on life-support equipment can join a vulnerable-customer list and are served first.

## Exposed or fallen live wires
Live wires are a danger to life. Keep away and warn others. A crew arrives within 2 hours. Also call the emergency services.

## Streetlights
Faulty streetlights are repaired within 7 working days. Streetlights at dangerous intersections are repaired within 3 working days.

## Meters and billing
Meter and billing queries go to the billing office and are answered within 10 working days.
